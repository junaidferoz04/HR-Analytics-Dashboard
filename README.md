# 📊 HR Analytics Dashboard

![Dashboard Preview](./Screenshot%202026-07-26%20215048.jpg)

## 📌 Project Overview
An interactive **HR Analytics Dashboard** developed in **Tableau** to analyze employee attrition patterns, evaluate key performance indicators (KPIs), and assist HR leadership in data-driven workforce planning. 

By analyzing employee demographics, job satisfaction ratings, education backgrounds, and age distributions, this dashboard provides clear insights into organizational attrition dynamics and retention trends.

---

## 🔑 Key Metrics & Business Insights
* **Total Employees:** 1,470
* **Attrition Count:** 237 employees
* **Overall Attrition Rate:** 16.12%
* **Active Workforce:** 1,233 employees
* **Average Employee Age:** 37 years

### 💡 Key Insights
1. **Department-wise Attrition:** Research & Development (R&D) accounts for the majority share of total attrition (**56.12%**), followed by Sales (**38.82%**), and HR (**5.06%**).
2. **Age Distribution:** The **25–34** age bracket experiences the highest overall attrition volume (112 employees), highlighting early-to-mid career turnover.
3. **Education Field Impact:** Employees from **Life Sciences** (89) and **Medical** (63) fields represent the largest volume of departures.
4. **Job Satisfaction Matrix:** Lower satisfaction levels (Rating 1 & 2) among Laboratory Technicians and Sales Executives correlate with higher attrition counts.

## 📊 Dashboard Components
* **KPI Header Cards:** Immediate overview of total headcount, attrition count, attrition rate, active workforce, and average age.
* **Department-wise Attrition (Donut Chart):** Proportional distribution of turnover across Sales, R&D, and HR departments.
* **No of Employee by Age Group (Histogram):** Distribution of employee counts across defined age bins.
* **Job Satisfaction Rating (Heatmap Matrix):** Attrition mapped across job roles and satisfaction levels (1 to 4).
* **Education Field wise Attrition (Bar Chart):** Categorical attrition breakdown based on academic disciplines.
* **Attrition Rate by Gender (Multi-Donut Matrix):** Detailed age and gender breakdown across five age groups (*Under 25*, *25–34*, *35–44*, *45–54*, and *Over 55*).
